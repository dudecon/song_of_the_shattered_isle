**Song of the Shattered Isle** concept art series

**Status:** In progress — Batch 1 (Watercraft) generated and placed. Subsequent batches to follow in review cycles.

---

## Style & Approach Guidance

**Core Visual Language (consistent with the rest of the series):**
- Epic painterly fantasy style matching **Mood Image.png** (dramatic cinematic lighting, rich organic textures, turquoise sea accents where appropriate, lush/rugged mythic atmosphere, ancient wonder, highly detailed digital painting).
- Pure artwork — no text, watermarks, or UI overlays (small discreet artist signature in corner is acceptable if it appears).
- High composition variation: close-up hero shots on items/gear, environmental context shots showing use, group/processional scenes for cultural pieces.

**Material & Tech Progression (critical for visual identity):**
- **Early / Native:** Organic, leaf-based, grass, simple wood, stone tools, very low-tech "thatched-roof native" survivors living among ruins.
- **Mid (Animal Products):** Integration of animal materials — Fur (insulation, softness, cold protection), Scales (armor, waterproofing, hardness), Bird (feathers, lightness, decoration), Squid (decorative elements, ink, flexibility).
- **Industrial / Bronze-Iron:** Heavier forged metal, riveted elements, controlled fire/heat influence. Can subtly reference the aesthetic language of the robot animals (18_Stove.jpg, 19_Metal.jpg, 20_Flame.jpg, 21_Construct.jpg) for tools, forges, and metal goods — but keep these at **personal / equipment scale**, not large architecture.
- **Ancient / Transcendent Tier:** Late-game materials (Ancient Palm, Ancient Metal, Ancient Diamond, Special Animal influence) feel magical, luminous, otherworldly. These should read as "second game" power — glowing, crystalline, harmonic, tied to the Special Animal and the restored Symphony state.

**What This Folder Is NOT:**
- Large-scale buildings, forts, harbors, mines, towers, bridges, or infrastructure → those live in **04_Structures**.
- Flora / fruits → **03_Flora**.
- Animals themselves → **01_Animals**.
- Islands → **02_Islands**.

**Focus here:** Personal-scale and vehicle-scale items that players would see on characters, in inventories, during events, or as "tech tree" milestones. Think: "what does the gear and cultural expression look like as the People advance?"

**Narrative / Path Variation (where it makes sense):**
- **Order path:** More rigid, uniform, fortified-looking gear and instruments. Martial drums and heavy plate. Controlled, hierarchical aesthetic.
- **Chaos / Eel path:** Wild, improvised, "long" and tangled elements, over-decorated or chaotic forms. Fluid, excessive interconnection in design.
- **True Symphony path (ideal):** Beautiful integration — gear and instruments that incorporate living elements (subtle Wood/Grass growth, animal partnership motifs), resonant and harmonious. Mixed human + evolved animal crews using the tech together.

**Key Animal Material Influences (reference these images when generating):**
- Fur line (13_Fur.jpg, 14_Frost.jpg) → insulation, softness, cold-weather gear.
- Scales (16_Scales.jpg) → armor, shoes, waterproofing, hard surfaces.
- Bird (04_Song_Bird.jpg, 05_Wind_Bird.jpg) → feathers, lightness, decorative elements, wind instruments.
- Squid (09_Squid.jpg) → decorative flair, color, flexibility, writing/painting tools.
- Special Animal (01_Special_Animal.jpg) → rainbow, transcendent, harmonious elements in highest tiers.
- Robot animals (18–21) → industrial metal language for tools and mid/late metal goods (subtle, equipment scale only).
