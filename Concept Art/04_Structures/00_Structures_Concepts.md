# 04_Structures — Concept Art

**Song of the Shattered Isle** concept art series

**Style Guidance:**
- Early game: Organic, thatched, wood, grass, simple stone — low-tech native feel mixed with lost high-tech ruins.
- Mid game: More substantial wood + stone construction, better engineering.
- Late game / Advanced: Heavy use of metal, brick, industrial elements, mechanical details. Reference the robot animal images (especially 18_Stove, 19_Metal, 20_Flame, 21_Construct) for heavier, more constructed, industrial, and hybrid mechanical styles.
- Narrative variation:
  - **Order path**: Rigid, fortified, monumental, hierarchical.
  - **Chaos/Eel path**: Overgrown, interconnected, chaotic, "long" tangled structures.
  - **True Symphony path**: Living/hybrid architecture — structures that incorporate growing Wood and Grass, feel resonant and alive.

All images use the established epic painterly fantasy style of the series (matching Mood Image.png and the rest of the Concept Art folders).
